Mark Schep has worked in data and AI since 2015, for more than 10 clients in different domains, as analyst, data scientist, data engineer and AI engineer. Mark Your Data gives leadership an outside view on its strategic goals: whether AI helps reach them, how it helps, and how to set up the programme that gets there.

> The question isn't whether to use AI. It's which of your goals AI moves, and how you get started.

## Where it can lead

Most strategy conversations end up in one of three directions, and Mark Your Data can build each of them:

-   **A data platform.** You need a foundation for your data, on the cloud you choose. See [data platforms](/services/data-engineering).
-   **Conversational analytics.** Your business has questions, and your dbt team has the definitions. See [Mark Your Data Assistant](/conversational-analytics).
-   **Content creation at scale.** You want AI to create content that stays correct and on brand. See [AI engineering](/services/ai-engineering) and [the bol case](/cases/bol).

## When an outside view helps

-   You're wondering whether to invest in a data platform, in AI, or in which one first.
-   You have more ideas for AI than time to try them, and no way to tell which ones are worth it.
-   You have use cases, but they're too vague to start on.

## What Mark Your Data does

**Strategy to use cases.** Mark Your Data starts from your strategic goals and finds where data and AI move them.

**Use cases to experiments.** Each use case is defined, refined and turned into an experiment with a time box and a measure of success, so it can be validated fast.

**Programme design.** Mark Your Data designs the programme or project around the experiments that work: the order of the work, the data it needs and the team it takes.

### A first conversation

You meet with Mark to see whether an experienced outside view would help.

### Data & AI offsite

The offsite is for companies that want to do more with data and AI, but lack the experience, knowledge or time to sort it out. The offsite turns your strategy into use cases, and your use cases into experiments with a time box and a measure. It's hosted by Mark Schep and Berend Dumas, who have delivered 20+ data and AI projects together. It also works as team building, and it's meant to be a fun day out.
