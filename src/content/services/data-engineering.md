Mark Your Data builds data and AI platforms that aren't tied to one vendor. The platform runs on open-source tools such as dlt and dbt wherever they do the job, instead of vendor-specific tooling. The same design works on Google Cloud, AWS and Azure, so moving to another cloud means changing the infrastructure, not rebuilding the platform.

> A platform that isn't locked to one vendor is a platform you can move, and one you can negotiate on.

## Built on experience across clouds

Mark Your Data has built a full platform in production on Google Cloud, at Winparts. Mark Schep has also done data work on AWS at Tikkie and on Azure at Snelstart. The platform designs for AWS and Azure are complete and ready to build.

## Ready for AI

The platform connects to AI tools, so AI use cases build on the same data the analysts use. At Winparts, the data platform became an AI platform that helps prevent returns. You can also extend the platform with [conversational analytics](/conversational-analytics), so colleagues can ask their questions against your dbt definitions.
